The same clear diagnosis at every shop

EVCore Studio gives the shops that service your e-bikes and scooters one way to record a diagnosis: the complaint, the readings checked against a stated range, the findings, and a report the rider can keep.

What every report records

  1. 1

    The complaint and symptoms

    What the rider reported, recorded the same way at every shop.

  2. 2

    Readings with their range

    Each measurement with its expected range and where the range came from: your service manual, a label or a datasheet.

  3. 3

    Findings, clearly graded

    Observed, suspected or confirmed. A confirmed cause always says how it was confirmed.

  4. 4

    The work and the parts

    Recommended work and parts, on a report the shop gives the rider.

Why it matters for your network

  • See what was actually measured and found, not just “replaced controller”
  • Ranges come from your own documentation, so shops check against your numbers
  • Suspected and confirmed causes are never mixed up
  • Riders get a clear record of the repair

Each shop keeps its records on its own computer. Studio runs on Windows 10 and 11.
